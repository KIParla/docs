// Canonical KIParla Jefferson symbol table.
// Included verbatim by the universal conventions page and reusable by module
// pages that need to show "the standard table plus our additions".
// Keep this list in sync with the prose sections of transcription::index.adoc.

[cols="1,2,4", options="header"]
|===
| Symbol | Meaning | Explanation

| `,`
| Weakly rising intonation
| At the end of a token, marks weakly rising intonation.

| `?`
| Rising intonation
| At the end of a token, marks rising intonation.

| `.`
| Falling intonation
| At the end of a token, marks falling intonation.

| `:`
| Prolonged sound
| A stretched sound, placed after the prolonged vowel or consonant. The number
  of colons reflects the length of the prolongation.

| `=`
| Prosodically linked tokens
| No gap or pause between the two tokens it joins (latching).

| `(.)`
| Short pause
| A brief interval, usually between 0.08 and 0.2 seconds.

| `TEXT`
| Higher volume
| Words in uppercase are pronounced louder than the surrounding speech.

| `TEX~`
| Interrupted token
| A trailing `~` marks a cut-off token, usually a false start.

| `°TEXT°`
| Lower volume
| Text between degree signs is pronounced more quietly than surrounding speech.

| `>TEXT<`
| Faster pace
| Text between inward-pointing arrows is spoken more quickly.

| `<TEXT>`
| Slower pace
| Text between outward-pointing arrows is spoken more slowly.

| `[TEXT]`
| Overlapping speech
| Text in square brackets overlaps with another speaker's bracketed text.

| `(TEXT)`
| Hard-to-understand span
| Text in round brackets is the transcriber's best guess at unclear speech.

| `w(x)w`
| Phonetically reduced form
| Round brackets inside or touching a word enclose sounds of the canonical form
  that were not pronounced (`c(io)è` = "cioè" heard as "cè"). Resolved to the
  canonical spelling when whitelisted; otherwise read as a hard-to-understand
  span.

| `xxx`
| Non-comprehensible sequence
| Unintelligible speech. The number of `x`s roughly matches the number of
  syllables.

| `((TEXT))`
| Non-verbal behaviour
| Double parentheses enclose transcriber comments and non-verbal behaviour
  (laughter, sighs, coughs, …), described in Italian. `((canta))` / `((legge))`
  close a sung or read unit; musical inserts, background noise and group actions go on a separate `environment` tier.

| `#word`
| Non-Italian token
| Marks a token that is certainly not Italian (dictionary test; geographic
  synonyms count as Italian and are not marked).

| `#_`
| Non-Italian unit
| At the start of a unit: the whole unit is in a code other than Italian.
|===
